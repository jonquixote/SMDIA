# Product Constitution

## Authority
This is the conflict-resolution authority for the document system. If a lower document conflicts with this Constitution, this Constitution prevails.

## Binding invariants
- Only native canonical records may determine authorization, discovery eligibility, entitlement, governance, or policy enforcement.
- A follow, graph distance, imported edge, interaction, community membership, payment, or ranking score never independently authorizes protected access or distribution.
- Private access is governed by `publication_snapshot_id`, which binds `policy_snapshot_id`, `audience_snapshot_id`, authorization timestamp, and applicable charter version.
- Private content, private metadata, audience records, attachment references, and topology data must never enter an adapter write queue.
- Recipient consent and preference constraints can narrow author-selected discovery or distribution.
- Ranking orders only material already eligible under the evaluator; it cannot authorize reach.
- Creator/customer payables are liabilities; only retained platform revenue enters company surplus accounting.
- No core mechanism may optimize a single proxy without explicit safety, quality, and anti-fraud constraints.

## Canonical identifiers
`policy_id`, `policy_schema_version`, `evaluator_semantics_version`, `policy_snapshot_id`, `audience_snapshot_id`, and `publication_snapshot_id` are the required identifiers for policy creation, enforcement, audit, analytics, and migration.

## Amendment
Constitutional changes require a dated decision record, linked implementation/migration plan, and explicit update to affected contracts and traceability rows.
