## Purpose

Separate platform control, economic upside, and resource-allocation rights; make creator terms legible; prevent creator/customer money from being confused with company surplus; and prevent unresolved cooperative aspirations from being marketed as guaranteed ownership.

## Creator Rights Default — CONSTITUTIONAL

- Creator retains copyright by default.
- Creator grants the platform a limited, revocable-as-operationally-possible license to host, secure, process, display, and distribute content according to selected policy.
- The platform claims no blanket AI-training, advertising, or unrelated commercial-reuse license.
- Collaborator revenue splits do not automatically transfer copyright.
- Community charters cannot override creator rights without clear affirmative agreement.
- Public cross-posting may create destination-specific licenses; users acknowledge them before external write.
- Takedown, dispute, and export procedures must be published before paid content launches.

Implements `CONST-ECON-002`.

## Early Commerce Stack — MVP POLICY

1. Tips
2. Pay-what-you-want posts/digital goods
3. Named collaborator revenue splits
4. Transparent creator earnings ledger
5. Community-fund allocation visibility

This is the first sharp edge: **earn how you want, split credit fairly, and see where money went.** Subscriptions, rentals, sponsorship, tickets, advanced licensing, AI-rights marketplace, and complex tax workflows are future design, not Phase 1 commitments.

## Accounting Order — CONSTITUTIONAL

1. A customer transaction states its price, direct processing cost, platform fee, refund rules, and collaborator split.
2. Creator net proceeds and collaborator allocations are liabilities payable to those parties; they are not company surplus.
3. Only retained platform revenue enters company operating expenses, reserve, reinvestment, investor-return policy, and possible participant/community-benefit allocation.

This distinction is required in product copy, ledger design, financial operations, and audit terminology.

## Fees and Value Flow

### Fee policy — CONSTITUTIONAL / OPEN DECISION

Fees, deductions, payout timing, and refund/chargeback terms are disclosed, versioned, and auditable. The actual fee schedule, cap, reserve target, and amendment process are **OPEN DECISION** items requiring finance/legal completion before public “capped extraction” marketing.

### Company retained-revenue waterfall — FUTURE DESIGN / LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

Subject to legal, tax, fiduciary, reserve, and contractual obligations, the company intends to allocate distributable retained revenue through transparent published rules that prioritize participant and community benefit over uncapped extraction:

1. Direct company operating expenses and worker compensation
2. Resilience reserve with published target/release policy
3. Capped, disclosed reinvestment budget
4. Investor return if applicable, under disclosed mission-compatible constraints
5. Participant/community-benefit allocation under approved rules

No percentage or payout expectation is implied until legal and financial decisions are finalized.

## Community Funds — FUTURE DESIGN / LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

Any community fund requires a defined source, budget limit, purpose, eligibility, conflict policy, voting/participation rule, operator compensation disclosure, allocation records, and appeal path. A fund is not an implied profit-share promise.

## Commerce Operations — MVP POLICY

Implementation must define payment authorization, payout schedule, taxes, refunds, chargebacks, disputes, fraud, entitlement lifecycle, digital fulfillment, collaborator-split failure handling, identity/payout verification, and portable ledger/tax records.

No payout/ranking/governance mechanism may be based solely on raw engagement, outrage, followers, invitations, or fabricated transactions (`CONST-METRIC-001`).

## Portability — CONSTITUTIONAL

| Asset | Export promise |
|---|---|
| Content | Source media, text, metadata, rights, revisions, and commerce records |
| Audience relationship | Consent-respecting connection record and opt-in channels only where permitted |
| Community role | Membership, charter, permitted contributions/decisions/entitlements |
| Earnings | Ledger, payouts, invoices, splits, licenses, tax documentation, entitlement history |

Users own their relationship to an audience, not others’ private data (`CONST-PORT-001`).

## Control and Governance — FUTURE DESIGN / LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED

Preferred direction: steward ownership plus a governed community-benefit pool. Phase 0 must resolve corporate form, mission lock, stakeholder rights, voting caps, delegation, quorum, appeals, transferability, redeemability, anti-capture rules, and amendment process. Token-first speculative pseudo-ownership is rejected.

## Legal Checklist

Corporate form; mission-lock enforceability; payment/money-transmission exposure; tax and creator payout rules; securities implications; consumer/refund law; copyright/licensing/AI rights; contributor contracts; privacy/portability; international obligations.
